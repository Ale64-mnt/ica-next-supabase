# Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026


This Cookies Policy explains what Cookies are and how they are used on this Website.

This Website uses a consent management platform (Cookiebot by Usercentrics) to manage Cookies and similar tracking technologies in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

## What are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on a user’s device when visiting a website. Cookies can be **session cookies**, which are deleted when the browser is closed, or **persistent cookies**, which remain stored for a defined period.

## How Cookies are used on this Website

This Website uses Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Strictly necessary cookies**  
  Required for the correct functioning, security, and basic operation of the Website.

- **Preference cookies**  
  Used to store user preferences and choices (such as language settings).

No statistics, analytics, marketing, advertising, or profiling cookies are used unless explicitly enabled by the user through the cookie consent banner.

## Cookie consent

When you first visit this Website, you are shown a cookie banner managed by Cookiebot.

- Cookies other than strictly necessary ones are **blocked by default**
- You can **accept, reject, or customize** your consent at any time
- Your consent choices are recorded and stored in accordance with GDPR requirements

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking on the **“Manage cookies”** link available on the Website.
